# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-06

Initial public release.

- **Étoile**: hashtag sampling with FR / UK / global market filters, language
  detection, creator heuristics, JSON/CSV export and a styled XLSX report.
- **Lumière**: single or paired video analysis — metadata, MP4 download,
  auto-captions, top comments, shot-cut statistics, 5-frame storyboard, and an
  eight-dimension Excel/Markdown report.
- macOS `.app` wrappers with native file/market pickers.
- Portable launchers (no hard-coded paths or account names).
